This website doesn't use advertising or tracking cookies, and it doesn't run analytics.

## What we do use

- **Form check:** the Help form and the school check use Cloudflare Turnstile to block bots. Turnstile may set short-lived, strictly necessary data to do that check.
- **Delete account page:** the sign-in code you enter is used only on that page, in memory, and isn't stored in a cookie.

The OnlySwap app stores your sign-in session on your phone so you stay signed in. That isn't a website cookie, and it's removed when you sign out or delete the app.

## Your choices

You can block or clear cookies in your browser settings. The form check may not work if you block it.
