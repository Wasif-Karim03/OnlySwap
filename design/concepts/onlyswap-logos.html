Six logo directions

Each one is a different idea, not six versions of the same arrow. Every card shows the app icon, how it holds up at tiny sizes (the 16px one is what a browser tab shows), and the full lockup with the wordmark. Use the colors at the top to see each one in any accent.

On a real home screen

All six sitting where students will actually see them, between other apps. The ones that still read clearly here, at 62px on a busy wallpaper, are the ones worth keeping. Detail that looks nice at 200px disappears at this size.
