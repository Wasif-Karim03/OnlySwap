# D. Rendering, startup, bundle (mobile)
Targets: 60 fps (120 ProMotion), cold start < 2 s on mid-range Android, no JS block > 100 ms. Measured FPS, TTI and JS-block times are NOT TESTED (need a device; no adb device, simulator app not running). React Compiler is on (`reactCompiler: true`, app.config.ts:296), which is why there is no `React.memo` anywhere and why inline closures are lower risk than usual.
