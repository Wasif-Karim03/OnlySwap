These can't be listed, offered or asked for on OnlySwap:

- Alcohol, vapes, nicotine and weed
- Medication, supplements and drugs
- Weapons, including pepper spray and knives
- Fake IDs, fakes and counterfeits
- Tickets, meal swipes and parking passes
- Coursework, exams and essays
- Pets and animals
- Gift cards and money transfers
- Recalled or unsafe items
- Stolen items
- Anything else that's illegal to sell where you are

Listings with these are removed. Repeat posts lead to a strike or a ban. If you're not sure, don't list it, or ask on the [Help page](/help).
