Everyone here agrees to these. Break them and your account comes down.

## 1. Meet in public

Use a Meetup spot or a busy place on campus. Not a dorm room, not an empty parking lot. Daytime is better.

## 2. Pay in person

Cash, Venmo, whatever you both agree on, after you've seen the item. OnlySwap never touches the money. Never pay before you meet, and never send a deposit.

## 3. Nothing on the banned list

No alcohol, vapes, meds, weapons, fakes, tickets, meal swipes or coursework. See the full [banned items list](/banned-items).

## 4. Keep chats about the deal

No harassment, threats, hate, sexual messages, or calling out other students. Anything creepy gets reported and looked at by a real person.

## Also

- Be honest about the item and its condition.
- Show up, or cancel in the app if you can't. Repeated no-shows pause your account.
- One account per person, and you must be 18 or older.

## What happens if rules are broken

A real person reviews every report. Outcomes range from a warning to a strike, a suspension or a permanent ban. You can appeal in the app. See section 6 of the [Terms](/terms).
